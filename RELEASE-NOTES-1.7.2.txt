ET: Legacy Frag Finder 1.7.2 by ght

Headshot action-boundary hotfix

- Headshots still means confirmed HIT_HEADSHOT events, not only kills whose
  obituary was marked as a headshot.
- The aggregate now includes confirmed headshots against eventual action
  victims during the five seconds leading into the first kill. This is the
  same lead-in shown by Play selected (-5s).
- The lead-in is cut at the selected player's most recent death, so hits from
  a previous life can never be attached to the next action.
- Headshots between the first and last kill, repeated headshots on one victim,
  Minimum headshots filtering, sorting, Folder scan, Highlights and exports
  continue to use the same corrected aggregate.
- Per-kill Event type rows remain labelled HEADSHOT KILL. This is separate
  from the action's total confirmed headshot-hit count.

before the first obituary. Minimum headshots 7 includes the action; 8 excludes

The SQLite per-hit index introduced in 1.7.1 is reused without reparsing.
